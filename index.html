Privacy Policy – Glamora

Last updated: February 2026

Glamora respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the Glamora mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide core app functionality:

  • Email address – used for account creation and login
  • Authentication data – securely managed through Firebase Authentication
  • Basic app usage data – to improve app performance and stability

We do not collect:

  • Payment information
  • Contact lists
  • Location data
  • Sensitive personal data

How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate users and manage accounts
  • Allow secure login and access to the app
  • Maintain and improve app functionality
  • Ensure security and prevent unauthorized access

Third-Party Services

Glamora uses trusted third-party services to operate the app:

  • Firebase Authentication – for secure user login
  • Firebase Analytics and Crash Reporting – to monitor app performance and stability

These services may collect information as described in their own privacy policies.

Data Storage & Security

  • All user data is stored securely using Firebase, a Google-provided service
  • Industry-standard security practices are used to protect your information
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties

User Rights

  • Access your account
  • Request account deletion
  • Request removal of your personal data

To request deletion, please contact us using the email below.

Children’s Privacy

Glamora is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy
